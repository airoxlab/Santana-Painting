# Santana Painting

Single-page website for Santana Painting — an owner-operated painting contractor serving
Laguna Hills and South Orange County, California.

- **Phone:** (323) 819-3605
- **Service area:** Laguna Hills, Mission Viejo, Aliso Viejo, Lake Forest, Laguna Niguel,
  Laguna Woods, Irvine, Rancho Santa Margarita
- **Status:** Licensed California painting contractor, $25,000 surety bond, sole owner,
  no employees (licensed July 2026)

Services: interior painting, exterior and stucco, cabinets and millwork, accent walls and
color consultation. Free written estimates.

## Structure

- `index.html` - the complete site (self-contained CSS, no build step)
- `img/` - 9 verified stock photographs of residential painting work, credited in the footer
- `screenshots/` - per-section screenshots of the rendered site (desktop + mobile)

Eight sections: hero, services, how it works, why us, the work, areas served, free estimate,
contact. Mobile-first, dark-mode aware.

## Sourcing note

This is a brand-new business with no online presence - no website, no Google Business Profile
photos, no social media. Every photo is verified stock, disclosed plainly in the footer as
representative rather than completed projects. No license number, review count, years in
business, or project claims are stated anywhere on the site, since none could be verified.
Not affiliated with Santana Painting Corp or the Woodside, CA Yelp listing - different companies.

Open `index.html` in a browser, or deploy the folder as-is to any static host.
